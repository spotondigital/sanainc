Our Attorneys

Meet the dedicated legal professionals behind SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc., committed to providing trusted legal advice, strategic representation, and exceptional client service.

Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo

Founder & Managing Director

Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo is the founding director of SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc. An admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa, he brings a unique and powerful blend of experience from both the corporate and public interest legal spheres.

His career includes pivotal roles at the Legal Resources Centre, where he honed his expertise in constitutional law and human rights, and at the South African Revenue Service (SARS) as a Tax Practitioner, where he gained an intimate understanding of tax law and administration. This rare combination allows him to approach commercial and civil matters with a keen awareness of both justice and regulatory compliance.

Mr. Nxumalo's practice is further grounded by hands-on experience in civil litigation, unlawful arrest, labour law, medical negligence, and Road Accident Fund claims, ensuring clients receive comprehensive and strategically astute legal counsel.

Anesu Zhandire

Associate

Anesu Zhandire is an Attorney at SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc. He is an admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa, with a practice grounded in civil litigation, estates administration, and drafting.

Prior to law, he spent several years at Amazon and AWS, where he gained valuable transferable skills. He holds a BSocSci in Criminology and Forensic Studies and an LLB from the University of KwaZulu-Natal.

Mr. Zhandire approaches each matter with strategic insight and careful attention to detail, ensuring clients receive comprehensive and well-considered legal counsel.
