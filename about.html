About SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc.

SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc. is a dynamic and forward-thinking South African law firm, founded on the principle that robust legal counsel is the cornerstone of both corporate success and individual justice. Established by Mr. Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo, the firm brings together a unique blend of deep commercial acumen, honed through years of experience as a Tax Practitioner, and a formidable litigation background, refined through his work in constitutional law, civil litigation, and labour law.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of legal excellence and ethical practice in Africa, recognised for shaping a future where justice and progressive corporate governance drive sustainable economic growth and empower communities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide exceptional, bespoke legal solutions that empower our clients—from corporations to individuals—to thrive within the South African and broader African context.

We achieve this by:

Justice & Governance

We champion the rule of law, advocate for our clients' rights with tenacity, and promote the highest standards of corporate governance as a catalyst for trust and long-term success.

Levaraging Deep-Seated Local Insight

Our practice is informed by the rich tapestry and unique socio-economic dynamics of South Africa, allowing us to offer advice that is not only legally sound but also contextually intelligent.

Fostering Collaborative Partnerships

We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients, founded on transparency, mutual respect, and a shared commitment to achieving transformative outcomes.

Driving Empowerment Through Knowledge

We are dedicated to the development of the legal sector on the continent, nurturing talent and contributing to a legacy of ethical and empowered African legal expertise.

Our Attorneys

Meet Mr. Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo, the founding attorney whose experience in commercial, constitutional, labour, and civil law forms the foundation of our legal practice.

Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo

Founder & Managing Lead Lawyer

Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo is the founding director of SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc. An admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa, he brings a unique and powerful blend of experience from both the corporate and public interest legal spheres.

His career includes pivotal roles at the Legal Resources Centre, where he honed his expertise in constitutional law and human rights, and at the South African Revenue Service (SARS) as a Tax Practitioner, where he gained an intimate understanding of tax law and administration. This rare combination allows him to approach commercial and civil matters with a keen awareness of both justice and regulatory compliance.

Mr. Nxumalo's practice is further grounded by hands-on experience in civil litigation, unlawful arrest, labour law, medical negligence, and Road Accident Fund claims, ensuring clients receive comprehensive and strategically astute legal counsel.
