About SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc.

SA Nxumalo Attorneys Inc. is a dynamic and forward-thinking South African law firm, founded on the principle that robust legal counsel is the cornerstone of both corporate success and individual justice. Established by Mr. Sicelo Alfred Nxumalo, the firm brings together a unique blend of deep commercial acumen, honed through years of experience as a Tax Practitioner, and a formidable litigation background, refined through his work in constitutional law, civil litigation, and labour law.
